Spendwise Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Summary

  • Your notes and amounts are stored on your device.
  • We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your information.
  • Spendwise has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.
  • Spendwise is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.
  • The app does not request internet access or any other Android permission.
  • Android's own backup feature may copy app data to your Google account if you have device backup turned on.

1. About this policy

This policy explains how the Spendwise mobile application for Android (package name com.luminescent.spendwise) handles information. Spendwise is published by Fluorescent Apes ("we", "us", or "our").

2. Information stored on your device

Spendwise lets you create notes for expenses and shopping lists. The following information is saved in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Note names
  • Item names and the amounts you enter, including savings goals
  • Web links you add to wish list items
  • Whether each item is checked
  • When each note was created and last changed
  • Unsaved changes to a note, so your work is not lost if the app closes
  • Your display preferences: full or compact view, and how notes are sorted

Your dark or light mode and language choices are saved on your device by the Android system.

Spendwise uses this information only to provide the app's features, such as showing your notes and calculating totals. All calculations happen on your device. Your notes may contain financial details, so please enter only what you are comfortable storing on your device.

3. Information we do not collect

We do not collect or have access to any information you enter into Spendwise. The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers, advertising ID, or usage data.

4. Permissions and internet access

Spendwise does not request any Android permissions, including internet access. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or other third-party software development kits (SDKs).

5. Android backup and device transfer

Spendwise allows the standard Android backup system to include its data. If you have device backup turned on, Android may copy your Spendwise data to your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new device. Android may also include this data when you transfer information directly from one device to another.

These features are provided by Android and Google and are controlled through your device and Google account settings. We cannot access these backups. Google's handling of backup data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. How information is shared

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your information with anyone, because we do not receive it. Google may process information when you download the app from Google Play, as described in Google's own privacy policy.

7. Keeping and deleting your information

Your information stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time:

  • An item: open the note, tap the trash icon next to the item, then save the note.
  • Notes: press and hold a note on the home screen, tap any other notes you also want to remove, tap Delete, then confirm.
  • All app data: uninstall Spendwise, or clear its storage in your device's app settings.

Backup copies are managed through your device backup and Google account settings.

8. Security

Spendwise stores your information in private app storage, which Android protects from other apps. No method of storage is completely secure, so we recommend protecting your device with a screen lock and keeping its software up to date.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information. Because we do not collect or hold information from the app, you can view, change, and delete your Spendwise data directly on your device. You may contact us with any questions about these rights.

10. Children's privacy

Spendwise is a simple list app suitable for people of all ages, including children. We do not collect, use, or share personal information from anyone, including children under 13 or under the minimum age set by the laws of your country.

The app has no accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, chat, or social features, and it does not request access to the internet, location, camera, contacts, or any other device feature. Anything a child enters stays on their device. Links in the app, such as the link to this privacy policy and web links added to a wish list, open in the device's web browser.

Parents and guardians can review or delete a child's notes directly in the app at any time, as described in section 7. If you have questions about your child's use of Spendwise, please contact us using the details below.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if the app's features change. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
